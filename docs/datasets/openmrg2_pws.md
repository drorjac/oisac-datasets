## Download (by hand)

No automatic download: preview: the PWS file is downloaded by the Download_PWS_data notebook from a Google Drive link.

Page: <https://github.com/OpenSenseAction/OpenMRG2>

Put the files in `$OISAC_DATA/openmrg2_pws/` (default `~/data/oisac-datasets/openmrg2_pws/`) and `python -m oisac_datasets describe openmrg2_pws` will describe them.
